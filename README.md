# Using-vehicle-body-to-make-it-work-like-EV
This project evaluates the feasibility of converting a Tata Nano ICE vehicle platform into an electric vehicle by performing EV powertrain and vehicle dynamics calculations. Gradient force, rolling resistance, aerodynamic drag, torque, and motor power requirements are analytically determined.
# EV Conversion Feasibility Study Using Tata Nano Platform

##  Project Overview
This project focuses on converting an existing Internal Combustion Engine (ICE) vehicle platform (Tata Nano) into an Electric Vehicle (EV) by performing powertrain and vehicle dynamics calculations. The study evaluates whether the vehicle can meet performance requirements such as acceleration, gradient climbing, and maximum speed when powered by an electric motor.

The work is based on analytical calculations related to EV powertrain design and drivetrain forces.

---

##  Objective
- To study the feasibility of converting an ICE vehicle body into an EV
- To calculate tractive effort and torque requirements
- To analyze vehicle performance on gradients
- To estimate motor torque and power requirements for EV operation

---

##  Vehicle Considered
- Vehicle Platform: Tata Nano (ICE body used as reference)
- Application: Electric Vehicle Conversion Study

---

##  Methodology
The EV conversion study was carried out using classical vehicle dynamics equations:

### Forces Considered
- Rolling Resistance Force  
- Aerodynamic Drag Force  
- Gradient Resistance Force  
- Acceleration Force  

### Total Tractive Force
The total tractive force required by the vehicle is calculated as the sum of all resistive forces.

### Torque Calculation
- Wheel torque calculated from tractive force and wheel radius
- Motor torque estimated using gear and differential ratios
- Starting torque calculated considering acceleration and gradient conditions

### Power Calculation
- Motor power estimated using torque and vehicle speed
- Maximum power requirement evaluated at top speed and gradient condition

##  Key Results

- Estimated starting torque requirement (~228.63 Nm)
- Motor power requirement calculated for EV operation
- Gradient climbing capability evaluated for real-world driving conditions
